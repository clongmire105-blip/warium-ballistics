Calculation model

Motion: Minecraft 1.20.1 AbstractArrow order, with position advanced before 0.99 air drag and 0.05 downward velocity are applied each tick.

RPL: Warium doubles battle-cannon and artillery launch velocity when Ritchie's Projectile Library is loaded. Mortar launch speed remains 5 blocks/tick.

Mortars: The displayed mortar pitch is solved through Warium's actual transformed launch vector. A 0-degree mortar setting produces approximately 45 degrees of true elevation when fired on its centerline.

Error: CEP50 and 90% miss radius use 800 deterministic trials of Minecraft's triangular projectile spread at the selected barrel or mortar inaccuracy.

Coordinates: One block is treated as one meter. VS local coordinates must be converted to world coordinates.

Limits: Terrain, collision, vehicle motion, server lag, and mount rotation limits are not modeled. Error figures are statistical estimates, not guarantees for an individual shot.

Derived from Warium 1.3.0 and Ritchie's Projectile Library 2.1.1 for Minecraft 1.20.1.
